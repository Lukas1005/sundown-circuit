# Sundown Circuit

A single-file browser racing game. Open `index.html` in Safari or Chrome, or play it online.

- Eight cars ranked by speed, with upgrades bought with coins collected on the road
- Seven tracks with 3D scenery, jumps, roller-coaster loops, boosters, oil spills and obstacles
- Drifting, reverse, boost, four camera views, checkpoints, minimap, music

Controls: arrows or WASD to drive, Shift to boost, Space to drift, R to restart from the last checkpoint, V to change camera, Esc to pause.
Progress is saved in the browser on the device you play on.
